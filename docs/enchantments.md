# Enchantments

Inspired by RotMG’s Equipment Rarity Update (May 2025): https://remaster.realmofthemadgod.com/?p=4486

RealmPack adapts the fixed slot, dust and modifier lock loop for short runs. Gear is created unattuned; the Enchanter fills all slots at once. Capacity is fixed: Uncommon 1 (60%), Rare 2 (28%), Legendary 3 (10%), Divine 4 (2%). Shiny gear has at least two slots; legacy gear retains every modifier and gains sufficient capacity. Existing dagger starting enchantments remain.

Runs start with 24 green, 16 red and 12 purple dust. Old saves without a dust bank receive the same supply once. A final combat victory grants 6 green, 3 + act index red and 2 + act index purple. Elite bonuses are 2/1/1; boss bonuses 4/2/2. Intermediate waves grant none.

Tiered weapons use green below T8, red T8–11, purple T12+. Armor boundaries are T9/T13, abilities and rings T4/T6. UT/ST use red for lower quality and purple for rare/legendary quality. A roll costs 8/12/16/20 of its dust color for 1/2/3/4 slots, doubled for every locked modifier. No growing reroll fee. Every unlocked slot rolls together. Locked modifiers stay; each roll is paid once and can be accepted or discarded. One item may be serviced per encounter. All locked disables rolling.

Ranks and strength stay I/II/III, 70%/25%/5%. Regular modifier weights are 10, Spark/Ash/Venom 7, Execution/Tactics/Shattering 4. New modifiers avoid duplicate types and competing categories: tempo (Might/Quickening/Execution), sustain (Restoration/Siphon), guard (Bulwark/Barrier/Warding), mana (Insight/Reservoir), proc (Spark/Ash/Venom/Shattering), retaliation (Thorns), tactics (Tactics). Existing modifiers are never rewritten; legacy conflicts can remain when locked. Artifact focusing and awakened modifiers are outside this adaptation.

Old paid single-slot gold previews can still be resolved without another fee. Dust, capacity, locks and pending comparisons save with the run. Clones copy capacity and modifiers.
